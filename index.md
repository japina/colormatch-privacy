# Privacy Policy for ColorMatch

*Last updated: August 2026*

This privacy policy explains what information ColorMatch ("the app") collects and how it is used.

## Summary

ColorMatch does not operate its own servers and does not collect, store, or sell any personal information itself. The app uses two third-party services — Apple Game Center and Google AdMob — which may collect limited data as described below.

## Information Collected

### Game Center (Apple)

If you sign in to Game Center, the app submits your move-count score to a Game Center leaderboard. This is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy — ColorMatch never sees or stores your Game Center identity or scores itself.

Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

### Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays banner ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect:

- Device and advertising identifiers
- Approximate location (derived from IP address)
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by ColorMatch's developer.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from apps that use Google services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

### Data Stored On Your Device

The app saves your current level progress locally on your device (using iOS's standard UserDefaults storage) so it can resume where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer.

## Children's Privacy

ColorMatch does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us using the information below so it can be removed.

## Your Choices

- You can decline to sign in to Game Center; the app functions fully without it.
- You can reset your device's advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your iOS Settings under Privacy & Security.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
