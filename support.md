# ColorMatch Support

Need help with ColorMatch, found a bug, or have feedback? Contact:
**info@japina.eu**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I play?**
Tap a colored square, then tap a neighboring one to swap them. If matching
colors end up next to each other, they disappear. Clear the whole board to
move to the next level.

**Why do I see ads?**
ColorMatch is free to play and supported by ads shown at the bottom of the
screen.

**How does the leaderboard work?**
Sign in to Game Center to submit your best (fewest-moves) score per level
and see how you compare to other players.

**Can I reset my progress?**
Currently progress is tied to your device automatically. If you'd like a
manual reset option added, let us know at the email above.

**I found a bug or have a feature request.**
Email us — we read every message.
